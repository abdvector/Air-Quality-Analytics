# Dataset attribution and licence

XKDR Forum (2026). **India Air Quality Database.** https://airquality.xkdr.org/

The compilation is released under **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International**: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Underlying sources: Central Pollution Control Board, Continuous Ambient Air Quality Monitoring (CAAQM) network; the full publisher compilation also includes U.S. Department of State/AirNow embassy monitors. **Only the CPCB subset is analyzed here.**

Changes made by this project: selection of 2024 and seven pollutants; spelling normalization of units; exclusion of analytical invalid values; duplicate conflict quarantine; IST/UTC normalization; daily summaries; coverage-aware rankings; derived ordinal bands. These changes and conclusions are the project's work, not endorsement by CPCB or XKDR Forum. Source data can be preliminary and is not validated here for regulatory decisions.

An alternative published-AQI mirror from https://github.com/Vonter/india-cpcb-aqi was inspected but its downloaded AQI archive ended in 2023. It is **not used in or distributed with the 2024 analytical outputs**. The original candidate download remains separate from the adopted measurement files.
